# Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE) Formulation

OEE measures manufacturing effectiveness across three distinct dimensions:

$$OEE = Availability \times Performance \times Quality$$

- **Availability**: Ratio of actual operating hours to planned shift hours (8.0h).
- **Performance**: Ratio of units produced to maximum theoretical capacity at rated cycle time.
- **Quality**: Ratio of defect-free units to total units produced.
